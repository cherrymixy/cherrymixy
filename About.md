# 🔍 About Me

Hi, I'm **SeungA (Cherry) Jeon**, a UX/UI & Product Designer exploring the intersection of **Design, AI, Mobility, and Emerging Technologies**.

I enjoy transforming complex technologies into experiences that feel intuitive, meaningful, and human-centered. Rather than focusing solely on interfaces, I am interested in how people interact with systems, environments, and intelligent technologies in their everyday lives.

My work often begins with a question:

> How can technology become a better experience for people?

This curiosity has led me to explore Human-AI Interaction, Physical AI, Future Mobility, AI-native products, and experimental interaction paradigms.

---

# 🚀 What I Do

### 🤖 Human-Centered AI Experiences

I explore how AI can move beyond tools and become meaningful interactions. Recently, I've been researching Physical AI, behavior-driven interactions, and AI-native user experiences.

### 🚗 Future Mobility & Emerging Technology

Through projects such as LOOPIN, I have explored how mobility can evolve from transportation into an adaptive experience that supports changing user contexts and everyday routines.

### 🧪 Experimental Product Making

I enjoy building experimental prototypes through AI-assisted workflows and vibe coding, using technology not only as a production tool but also as a medium for exploration and rapid validation.

### 📊 Product Thinking & UX Strategy

My process combines user research, information architecture, interaction design, design systems, and product thinking to transform ambiguous problems into structured experiences.
